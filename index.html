About me

Natalia Madrid is a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of California San Diego, and a Fulbright Scholar. Her research studies how financial markets, particularly banking and insurance, help households and firms adapt to climate risk. Her work has recently been recognized with the Richard Libby Graduate Award from the Rady School of Management and the Benjamin C. Horne Memorial Prize from the Department of Economics at UC San Diego.

In the summer of 2026, she was a research intern in the General Directorate of Financial Stability at Banco de México. Before her Ph.D., she earned a Bachelor's degree in Engineering Sciences and a Master's degree in Financial Management in Chile.
